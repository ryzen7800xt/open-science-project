Sulfur Dioxide (SO₂)

What Human Activities Produce Sulfur Dioxide?

Humans produce Sulfur Dioxide by burning coal, oil, or melting ores like iron. It is known for affecting human health, and creating a thick haze.

Does the polluntant create a suspension, colloid, or a solution?

Sulfur dioxide forms a solution when it dissolves in water, creating acid.

At what concentration is it harmful?

Sulfur dioxide is harmful at concentrations of 0.5 parts per million (ppm) or higher.
